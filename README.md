## Introduction

In this project, we conducted an in-depth case study on **Valve Corporation**, focusing on its organizational structure, culture, and cybersecurity challenges. The aim was to identify strategies for enhancing cybersecurity within the company without disrupting its unique flat structure.

Through comprehensive analysis and strategic planning, we proposed a **Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (CoE)** as a solution that balances the need for improved security with Valve's culture of autonomy and innovation.

---

## Project Overview

Valve, a leading video game developer and digital distribution company, is known for its flat organizational structure and strong company culture, emphasizing freedom and creativity. However, like many organizations, Valve faces increasing cybersecurity challenges, especially with the rise of cyber threats targeting the gaming industry and its digital platforms.

Our project involved:
1. **Analyzing Valve's Structure and Culture**: We studied Valve's flat hierarchy and culture, which fosters innovation but also poses challenges for traditional cybersecurity management.
2. **SWOT Analysis**: We performed a SWOT analysis to evaluate Valve's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats related to its cybersecurity posture.
3. **Cybersecurity Strategy Proposals**: We proposed several cybersecurity approaches, ultimately focusing on the creation of a **Center of Excellence for Cybersecurity**.
4. **Comparative Analysis with ENISA**: To validate our approach, we drew an analogy with **ENISA (European Union Agency for Cybersecurity)**, which provides strategic cybersecurity insights and training without having decision-making control over national security policies.

---

## Proposed Solution: Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (CoE)

The **Center of Excellence for Cybersecurity (CoE)** would be a dedicated unit within Valve that:
- Provides strategic insights into cybersecurity without disrupting Valve's existing structure.
- Acts as a center for **training** and **knowledge-sharing** across the company.
- Collaborates with external cybersecurity bodies (e.g., ENISA) to ensure that Valve stays at the forefront of emerging cybersecurity trends and best practices.
- Offers **advisory services** to the development teams while respecting Valve’s decentralized decision-making process.

---

## Key Objectives of the CoE:
- **Enhance Cybersecurity Awareness**: Provide ongoing training and strategic insight for all employees.
- **Facilitate Collaboration**: Work with other teams and departments to improve cybersecurity practices across the board.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Keep up with the latest cybersecurity developments and adapt strategies accordingly.

We believe that by adopting this model, Valve can bolster its cybersecurity capabilities while preserving its organizational culture and operational autonomy.

---

## Why Valve?

Valve is unique in its approach to business and organization. With its flat structure and open culture, any changes must respect the company's philosophy of **freedom**, **creativity**, and **independence**. Our proposed cybersecurity CoE model aligns with this culture by offering cybersecurity enhancements without enforcing top-down decision-making or disrupting Valve's well-established organizational methods.

We are proud to have developed a solution that fits seamlessly into Valve's existing framework, offering an effective yet non-intrusive approach to improving its cybersecurity posture.
